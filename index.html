About Lace Industries

For nearly four decades, Lace Industries has been the trusted partner for premium lace and textile manufacturing in India. Based in Narasapuram, West Godavari District, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern production techniques to deliver exceptional quality.

Our commitment to excellence extends across every product we manufacture. From intricate lace patterns to fine textiles, we serve fashion designers, retailers, and wholesalers across India and internationally.

We pride ourselves on understanding our clients' unique needs and delivering customized solutions that exceed expectations.
